AWK – Introduction & Working
OPERATING SYSTEMS
●Scripting language for:
○Pattern scanning & processing
●Works line-by-line
●Used for:
○Data filtering
○Report generation
Working
●Reads file line by line
●Splits line into fields
●Applies:
○Pattern → Action
