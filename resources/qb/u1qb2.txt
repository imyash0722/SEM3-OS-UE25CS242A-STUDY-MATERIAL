   operations. Analyze the complete impact on CPU utilization, I/O performance, and

   to the Convoy Effect. Analyze this phenomenon and explain why FCFS is generally

   Explain the metric by which it is considered optimal, and discuss the primary
   implementation challenge that prevents its direct use.

   and 3ms. Compare the Average Waiting Time if scheduled in different orders. What

   criteria even if the scheduling algorithm itself makes optimal decisions?

   exponential averaging to predict it. If the prediction is consistently wrong for a
   process whose burst pattern changes suddenly, how does this affect the scheduling
   decisions?

   burst is currently running and a high-priority I/O-bound process enters the ready

   unordered linked list. How does the choice of data structure affect the
   scheduler's performance for FCFS versus SJF?

   preemptive SJF and what fundamental scheduling goal is violated.
